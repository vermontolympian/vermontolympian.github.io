About

I'm a robotics engineer with a BS and MS in Robotics Engineering from Worcester Polytechnic Institute. My work spans real-time control, PLC and embedded systems, kinematics, and hardware validation — with a focus on turning research-stage systems into reliable, production-ready equipment.
