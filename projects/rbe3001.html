RBE 3001

Unified Robotics I — Worcester Polytechnic Institute

Focused on the foundations of robotic manipulation: forward and inverse kinematics, Jacobians, and closed-loop control of a robotic arm.

Work included modeling arm kinematics, implementing trajectory and motion control, and integrating computer vision for object detection and pick-and-place tasks.
