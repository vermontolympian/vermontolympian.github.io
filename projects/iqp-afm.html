Microtechnology Project: PBL Module Development

Interactive Qualifying Project · March – October 2021

Led interviews, research, and development of project-based learning materials to increase hands-on understanding of Atomic Force Microscopy, working directly with professors and students at the Eastern Switzerland University of Applied Sciences (OST) in Buchs, Switzerland.

We interviewed students and professors at both WPI and OST to gather data that shaped the module. In it, OST students receive a rubric to guide research, manufacturing, and creation of their own atomic force microscopy probes.
